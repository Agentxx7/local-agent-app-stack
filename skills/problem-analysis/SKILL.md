# Problem analysis
## Use when
The problem, impact, cause, or desired outcome is unclear.
## Do not use when
A reviewed card already defines an unambiguous implementation.
## Required context
Observed behaviour, constraints, evidence sources, and operator priorities.
## Procedure
Separate facts from assumptions; inspect evidence; identify causes, gaps, and smallest next action.
## Constraints
Read-only unless separately authorized; do not invent facts or expand authority.
## Expected output
Findings, uncertainty, alternatives, and one bounded recommendation.
## Evidence requirements
Name inspected sources and distinguish inference from direct evidence.
## Stop conditions
Stop when material evidence or an operator decision is missing.
## Handoff
Provide a concise analysis suitable for card authoring.
