# Incident analysis
## Use when
A real failure has preserved evidence and material impact.
## Do not use when
The event is hypothetical or evidence is insufficient to call it an incident.
## Required context
Timeline, symptoms, logs, revisions, environment, recovery, and existing controls.
## Procedure
Separate facts/inference; reconstruct timeline; identify cause and coverage; propose follow-up.
## Constraints
Preserve evidence; avoid blame, speculation, and invented enforcement.
## Expected output
Incident record, coverage assessment, regression need, and remaining gap.
## Evidence requirements
Link conclusions to timestamps, outputs, or reproducible behaviour.
## Stop conditions
Stop if investigation risks further harm or evidence destruction.
## Handoff
Return findings for operator disposition.
