# Evidence and status

Use precise states such as `NOT_STARTED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `BLOCKED`, `IMPLEMENTED_UNVERIFIED`,
`VERIFIED_AWAITING_OPERATOR`, `ACCEPTED`, `PARKED`, or `REJECTED`. `KLAR` is permitted only when
relevant evidence is tied to the correct revision and the operator has explicitly accepted it.
Documentation, configuration, test presence, runtime availability, and proven capability are
different claims and must not be collapsed.
