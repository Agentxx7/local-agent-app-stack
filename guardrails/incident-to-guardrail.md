# Incident to guardrail

- Rule: A confirmed incident produces a guardrail, regression test, or documented operator decision.
- Purpose: Convert failure evidence into reusable prevention or detection.
- Applies when: An incident has sufficient evidence and an established failure mechanism.
- Prohibited behaviour: Closing incidents without disposition or inventing controls not implemented.
- Required evidence: Impact, root cause, recovery, existing coverage, new measure, and remaining gap.
- Enforcement type: OPERATOR DECISION.
- Operator override: The operator may accept no new control when the rationale is recorded.
- Remaining limitations: Lessons depend on accurate incident analysis and maintained checks.
