# Rejected material policy

- Rule: `REJECTED` material is absent from active code, config, state, cache, assets, and fallback.
- Purpose: Ensure rejection is a real lifecycle outcome rather than a label.
- Applies when: The operator rejects or permanently removes a capability or artifact.
- Prohibited behaviour: Active aliases, fixtures, dispatch, compatibility routes, or hidden fallback.
- Required evidence: Repository, state, cache, asset, runtime, and reference absence checks.
- Enforcement type: OPERATOR DECISION.
- Operator override: Preservation belongs in history or explicit incident evidence, not active paths.
- Remaining limitations: Complete removal cannot be proven by one generic scan.
