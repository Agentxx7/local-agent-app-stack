# Destructive command policy

- Rule: Destructive rollback or deletion requires exact targets, verified provenance, and authority.
- Purpose: Avoid irreversible loss of user work, data, and evidence.
- Applies when: Deleting, overwriting, force-updating, restoring, or rolling back state.
- Prohibited behaviour: Broad destructive commands, unresolved targets, or rollback without a recovery path.
- Required evidence: Exact target, ownership, snapshot or recovery method, and operator authorization.
- Enforcement type: WRITTEN RULE.
- Operator override: The operator may authorize a precise operation after reviewing consequences.
- Remaining limitations: A written policy cannot intercept commands unless a project adds tooling.
